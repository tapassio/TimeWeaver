# Skill Category
A broad grouping of related skills within a specific area of expertise.

# Skill Topic
A specific subject or competency covered within a skill category.

# Skill Level
Indicates the degree of proficiency or expertise, spanning three levels:
1. Introduction (I): Basic awareness or familiarity with the competency.
2. Regular (R): Can apply the competency independently in routine situations.
3. Master (M): Demonstrates advanced expertise and can handle complex situations or guide others.
